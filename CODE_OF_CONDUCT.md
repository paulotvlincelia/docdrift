# Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

DocDrift welcomes people with different backgrounds, disciplines, languages, experience levels, and perspectives. We are committed to a community where participation is respectful, constructive, and safe.

## Expected behavior

- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Be precise without being dismissive.
- Explain assumptions and disclose uncertainty.
- Give credit for data, research, code, and prior work.
- Respect privacy, licenses, confidentiality, and requests to remove data.
- Make space for newcomers and help them build context.
- Accept correction and repair harm when mistakes happen.

## Unacceptable behavior

- harassment, threats, discrimination, or sexualized attention;
- personal attacks, insults, or sustained disruption;
- publishing private information without permission;
- knowingly submitting stolen, proprietary, poisoned, or mislicensed data;
- misrepresenting evaluation results, provenance, or authorship;
- retaliation against someone who raises a good-faith concern.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with appropriate confidentiality. Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue warnings, restrict participation, or ban participants when necessary.

Maintainers are held to the same standard and must recuse themselves when they are directly involved in a report.

## Scope

This code applies in repository spaces, project events, and public interactions where someone represents DocDrift.
